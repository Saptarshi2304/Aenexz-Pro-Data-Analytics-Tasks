# Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition using HR data to identify patterns and factors associated with employee turnover. The analysis was conducted using Tableau, with data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and business recommendations.

## Interactive Tableau Dashboard
**[View the Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/EmployeeAttritionAnalysis_17916328882090/EmployeeAttritionDashboard?:language=en-US&publish=yes&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)**

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Who Left: 237
- Active Employees: 1,233
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%

## Key Insights
- Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate than employees not working overtime.
- The Sales department had the highest attrition rate among the three departments.
- Employees under 25 had the highest attrition rate among the age groups analyzed.
- Employees in the lowest monthly income group showed higher attrition.
- Lower job satisfaction and work-life balance levels were associated with higher attrition.

## Recommendations
- Review overtime and workload distribution.
- Strengthen retention efforts in high-attrition departments and job roles.
- Improve onboarding, mentoring, and career development for younger employees.
- Review compensation and career-growth opportunities.
- Improve employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
